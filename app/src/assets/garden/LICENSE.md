# Garden placeholder sprites

All images in this folder are placeholder art from [Kenney](https://www.kenney.nl), licensed
**CC0 1.0 (public domain)** — http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Credit is not
required; it's given here anyway.

They are cropped to their opaque bounds; nothing else is changed. One sprite per archetype
(see `Archetype` in `src/domain/species.ts`), to be replaced later by baked archetype art.
