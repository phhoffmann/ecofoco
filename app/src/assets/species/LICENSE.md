# Species photos

Each `<id>.webp` is a photo from a research-grade iNaturalist observation, licensed **CC0** or **CC BY**
by its photographer. Changes from the source: iNaturalist's "medium" size (500 px long edge) scaled to at
most 480 px and re-encoded as WebP.

The photographer, licence and source observation of every photo are recorded in the species' `photo`
field in `src/catalog/<biome>.json` and listed in the app under Settings → Credits.

Generated by `npm run build:catalog` (see `app/README.md`); do not edit by hand.
